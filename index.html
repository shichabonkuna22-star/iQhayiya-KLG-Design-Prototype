A design-orientated firm working across South Africa.

iQhayiya Design Workshop (PTY) Ltd. is a professional architectural firm established and led by Kayalethu Qwalela and Mfundo Maphumulo. The studio serves municipalities and government, private developers and home owners. The practice includes precinct planning and urban design, and supervision of the building project. The studio works from 88 Marine Drive, Margate, KwaZulu-Natal.
